? What these labels mean
Company describes the employer category, such as Big Tech, Scale-up, or Growth SaaS. Function describes the job's main area of work. Visa sponsorship is a company-level likelihood based on sponsorship history or international hiring signals, and is never a guarantee. Curated target means a company career page worth monitoring, not a confirmed vacancy. Added shows when a live posting was first seen. No longer listed means the source no longer shows the posting.
Tracking Methodology

Live Job Index tracks active roles from top company careers pages and leading public job boards for people already in major hiring markets and international candidates looking for visa-sponsored paths into those countries. It organizes opportunities across the United States, UK, Ireland, Canada, Australia, Singapore, Germany, the Netherlands, Switzerland, the Nordics, Spain, Portugal, Estonia, New Zealand, France, Italy, Poland, Belgium, Finland, Austria, Japan, South Korea, India, and Taiwan, covering departments from engineering and product to sales, marketing, finance, operations, customer success, people, legal, and strategy. Visa likelihood reflects company-level sponsorship history and hiring signals, but it remains a prioritization heuristic and does not guarantee sponsorship or immigration eligibility.
