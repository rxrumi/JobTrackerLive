Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Live Job Index is operated by Sohaib Kazmi. This policy explains what information the service collects, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

Information We Collect

When you create an account or sign in, we collect your email address, name if provided by you or your Google account, authentication identifiers, and session information needed to keep you signed in. If you complete onboarding, we store the profile details you submit, such as target roles, countries, seniority, visa needs, work preferences, and optional links.

When you use the tracker, we store job statuses, starred jobs, notes, timestamps, and basic activity events needed to operate your personal pipeline.

Résumé Studio and AI Processing

If you use Résumé Studio, we collect the PDF or DOCX files you upload, career evidence you add or verify, master résumé settings, pasted job descriptions and URLs, generated résumé and email drafts, claim-audit results, versions, exported files, notification preferences, and usage records. Files and generated artifacts are stored in private Cloudflare storage and are delivered only through authenticated account endpoints.

Résumé and job content may be sent to OpenAI through Cloudflare AI Gateway to extract structured evidence, analyze job requirements, draft content, and audit claims. Requests are configured not to be stored by the model provider. Source files uploaded temporarily to the provider are deleted after extraction, including after failed extraction attempts. Live Job Index does not intentionally include résumé text, evidence, job-description text, or generated email bodies in application logs.

Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, Google shares basic profile information such as your name and email address with Clerk so Live Job Index can create or access your account. The app does not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts, or other Google account data.

Analytics and Consent

Essential cookies support authentication, security, consent choices, and account settings. Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and first-party behavioral analytics remain disabled unless you explicitly allow analytics. Global Privacy Control is treated as a denial. Clarity inputs and all onboarding, profile, and Résumé Studio surfaces are masked. You can withdraw consent at any time through “Privacy choices.” Analytics data is used to improve the service and is never sold.

How We Use Information

  • To authenticate users and maintain account sessions.
  • To save onboarding, profile, job status, notes, and pipeline state.
  • To operate and improve the job tracker and its public job feed.
  • To create user-requested résumé and email drafts, validate evidence citations, render files, and perform quality checks.
  • To recommend new job matches and, only when you opt in, prepare a limited number of automatic application packs.
  • To send opted-in transactional daily digests and build-status notifications. Résumé Studio never sends job outreach emails.
  • To protect the service from misuse and debug reliability issues.

Service Providers

The service uses Clerk for authentication; Cloudflare for hosting, D1, KV fallback, private R2 storage, queues, workflows, Worker-native document rendering, AI Gateway, and transactional email; OpenAI for configured AI processing; Google Analytics for consented usage measurement; and Microsoft Clarity for consented, masked interaction analytics. These providers process data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Data Retention

Account, pipeline, résumé, evidence, build, artifact, and entitlement data is kept while your account is active or needed to provide the service. Anonymous raw analytics is retained for up to 30 days, identified raw analytics for up to 90 days, and non-identifying aggregates for up to 13 months. Google Analytics retention is configured for two months and Clarity for 30 days. Temporary export archives expire after 24 hours.

Exports and Account Deletion

You can request a private account export from Settings. The durable export includes account records and original private files and is available through an authenticated download for 24 hours. You can also request deletion from Settings after recent authentication and typed confirmation. Deletion locks new mutations, revokes sessions, fences active workflows, verifies deletion of provider and private files, removes identifiable analytics and account data, deletes the Clerk identity, and retains only a non-identifying completion ledger.

Notifications and Choices

In-app recommendations are created only for enabled build rules. Daily email digests are optional and can be disabled in Résumé Studio or through the signed one-click unsubscribe link in each digest; login is not required for that unsubscribe action.
